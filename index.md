# Hi, I'm Nyra.

I'm a mechanical engineer who loves working at the intersection of mechanical design, electronics, and software. I'm happiest when I'm building something, figuring out how it works, or troubleshooting why it doesn't.

My experience spans everything from developing Mars rovers and testing rocket hardware to building robots and experimenting with computer vision. I love projects that push me into unfamiliar territory, especially when I get to work alongside people who are just as excited to figure things out.

Outside of engineering, I love getting outdoors, spending time with my dog, and finding new hobbies to try. I'm always excited to meet people who are passionate about what they do.
